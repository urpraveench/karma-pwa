## 1.8.0 · 10 October 2026 · 100 minutes a day for your productive life

- **Help adding TikTym to your home screen.** After your first minutes, a card shows how on your phone: one Install button on Android, the two Safari steps on iPhone, or how to open TikTym in your browser if a link opened inside WhatsApp or Instagram. It's also in Setup any time.
- **A new promise: 100 minutes a day for your productive life.** 30 minutes to move, 30 to read, 30 to grow and 10 to breathe, the four practices every new account starts with. Busy days still carry forward.
- **Notifications and privacy in Setup**: switches for each kind of email (tips and reminders, weekly recap, news) and for usage statistics. They're on from the start, and each turns off with one tap.
- **A welcome email** when you start, with the practices you chose and how to add TikTym to your home screen, and a gentle nudge a day later if you haven't logged your first minutes. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
- **Updated Privacy Policy and Terms** explaining what's on and how to turn it off.

## 1.7.2 · 9 October 2026 · Small things from your feedback

- **Follow TikTym on LinkedIn** from Setup › About, for tips and updates.
- **A running timer now says "12 min"** on its button in Today, instead of "12′". Thanks, [Gopi Aitham](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gopi-aitham/), for pointing it out.
- **What's new** in Setup › About opens this page.

## 1.7.1 · 9 October 2026 · Google sign-in comes first

- **Continue with Google** is now the main button on the sign-in screen: two taps, nothing to type. Email and password are still there, just below.

## 1.7.0 · 9 October 2026 · Choose what you want to make time for

![The "What do you want to make time for?" screen, with practices ticked and minutes beside each](media/first-run.jpg)

- **New accounts start by choosing their practices**: pick from suggestions like Course study, Reading, Exercise, Coding practice or Language learning, or add your own.
- **Set the daily minutes for each** with − and + before you begin. You can change everything later in Setup.

## 1.6.2 · 8 October 2026 · No more blank screen on slow connections

- **TikTym shows a loading screen straight away**, and tells you if your connection is slow, offline, or worth a reload, instead of a blank page.
- **The first screen appears sooner**, without waiting for fonts to download.

## 1.6.1 · 8 October 2026 · Nothing lost when you close the app

- **Minutes you log just before closing the app are always kept**, and saved to your account the next time it opens.

## 1.6.0 · 8 October 2026 · Edit entries and reorder practices

![Editing an entry: practice, day, minutes and note, with Save changes and Delete entry](media/edit-entry.jpg)

- **Edit a logged entry**: tap it in History to change its practice, day, minutes or note.
- **Reorder your practices** with the up and down arrows in Setup. Today follows the new order.
- **A new home: app.tiktym.com.** The old address still works and sends you there.
- **Easier to read in dark and light mode**, with better contrast across every screen.

## 1.4.0 · 8 October 2026 · Share your progress

- **A progress card** in Insights: this month's time, your streak and top practices, ready to share on LinkedIn or WhatsApp, with practice names hidden if you prefer.

## 1.3.0 · 8 October 2026 · Your privacy, your choice

- **Usage statistics only with your permission**: an unticked box when you sign up, and a switch in Setup › Privacy to change your mind at any time.

## 1.2.0 · 8 October 2026 · Feedback and better links

- **Send feedback** from Setup › About, straight to the people who build TikTym.
- **Links to TikTym look good** when shared on WhatsApp, LinkedIn and X.
- **The goal chime plays right on time** on iPhone.

## 1.0.0 · 8 October 2026 · TikTym launches

- **One minute, one point.** Set a small daily target for each practice and log minutes with one tap, or let the focus timer log them for you.
- **Busy weeks carry forward, but never pile up.** Missed minutes carry into the next day, up to a limit you choose.
- **Insights** show your streaks, consistency, and the time of day you practise best.
- **Works on any phone and offline.** Add it to your home screen; your data syncs to your account and is stored in India.
