# TikTym outreach

Your go-to-market outreach: what to do, in what order, and a log of what happened. GTM.md has the strategy; this file is the day-to-day work. Launched on LinkedIn on Thursday 8 October 2026.

**How to use it**

1. Work down **This week's tasks**; tick each box when done.
2. Every time you share TikTym anywhere, add a row to the **Share log** the same day.
3. Every Monday, do the **Weekly review** and plan the next week's tasks.

Use first names or initials for people, never phone numbers or full names, even though the repo is private.

## The plan in one line

LinkedIn profile first, then one-to-one WhatsApp messages, then Status, then one group every few days. Personal messages convert best; groups convert worst, so they come last, one at a time, never all at once.
