Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 October 2026

In short: TikTym stores your account details and the practices and minutes you log, so it can show your progress on any device. Your data is stored in India (Mumbai). We don't sell it, show ads, or use analytics or tracking. You can export or delete everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

TikTym is run by Pramal IT Services Private Limited (CIN U72900AP2022PTC121639), registered in Andhra Pradesh, India. We are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). Contact: praveen.cherukuru@gmail.com.

3. What stays on your device

To work quickly and offline, the app keeps some data in your browser's storage on your device: the app's files, a copy of your synced data, your sign-in session, your theme choice, a running focus timer, and small preferences such as tips you've dismissed. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it from that device; your account data stays safe in the cloud.

4. Our legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account and use TikTym, for the purposes above. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see section 7). Withdrawing doesn't affect processing that happened before.

5. Where your data is stored, and who processes it

  • Your account data and practice data are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Mumbai, India, using Firebase by Google.
  • Our processor is Google, which provides Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Hosting, Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA, and Google Fonts. Some of these services, such as sign-in, reCAPTCHA and fonts, may process data on Google's servers outside India, as the DPDP Act permits.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

This site is protected by reCAPTCHA, and the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. If you delete an entry or a practice, it's removed from our database straight away. If you delete your account, all your practices, entries, settings and your sign-in account are deleted. Our providers may hold copies in their own secured logs or backups for a limited time under their policies.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access a summary of your data and how it's processed. You can see all of it in the app, and download it with Setup › Data › Export as JSON.
  • Correct or update it: edit or delete practices and entries in the app, or ask us.
  • Erase it: delete entries and practices in the app, or everything with Setup › Danger zone › Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity, by writing to us.
  • Raise a grievance with us (section 11). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each signed-in person read and write only their own data, and App Check helps make sure only the real TikTym app can reach it. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach affects you, we'll inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9. Children

TikTym is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly process children's data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change; the date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or grievances about your personal data, write to our Grievance Officer at praveen.cherukuru@gmail.com. We'll acknowledge your message and respond as quickly as we can, and always within the time the law requires.
