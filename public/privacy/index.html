Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

In short: TikTym stores your account details and the practices and minutes you log, so it can show your progress on any device. Your data is stored in India (Mumbai). We don't sell it or show ads. Your account comes with emails about your practice (a welcome, first-week tips, a weekly recap and occasional news) and usage statistics through Google Analytics; each is on from the start, and you can turn each off at any time in Setup or from any email. You can export or delete everything at any time.

1. Who is responsible for your data

TikTym is run by Pramal IT Services Private Limited (CIN U72900AP2022PTC121639), registered in Andhra Pradesh, India. We are the Data Fiduciary for your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"). Contact: praveen.cherukuru@gmail.com.

2a. Anonymous daily totals

To see how TikTym is used overall, the app adds to a few running totals for each day, for everyone combined: how many devices were active, how many accounts were created, how many entries were logged (and whether by a quick tap, the timer and so on), how many minutes, how many timers were started and finished, how many practices were added and how many progress cards were shared. Only these numbers are sent. Nothing that identifies you or your device is included, and the totals can't be linked back to anyone, so they aren't personal data. To count each device once a day, the app keeps only the date it last counted itself, on your device.

3. What stays on your device

To work quickly and offline, the app keeps some data in your browser's storage on your device: the app's files, a copy of your synced data, your sign-in session, your theme choice, a running focus timer, and small preferences such as tips you've dismissed. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes it from that device; your account data stays safe in the cloud.

4. Our legal basis

We process your personal data on the basis of the consent you give when you create an account, after the sign-up screen tells you what an account includes, and for the purposes you provided it for (sections 6 and 7 of the DPDP Act). An account includes the emails about your practice and usage statistics described above: each is on from the start, and you can turn each off at any time in Setup › Notifications and privacy, or with the unsubscribe link in any email. Our website counts visits with Google Analytics from your first visit and shows a notice with an Opt out button. Phone reminders start only if you allow notifications in your browser. Turning something off stops it from then on; it doesn't affect processing that happened before (see section 7).

5. Where your data is stored, and who processes it

  • Your account data and practice data are stored in Google Cloud Firestore in Mumbai, India, using Firebase by Google.
  • Our processor is Google, which provides Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Hosting, Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Cloud Functions, Google Fonts and Google Analytics. Some of these services, such as sign-in, reCAPTCHA, fonts and Analytics, may process data on Google's servers outside India, as the DPDP Act permits.
  • Emails are sent by our processor Resend (Resend, Inc., USA), which receives your email address, first name and the content of each email (for a weekly recap, your own totals) only to deliver it.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

This site is protected by reCAPTCHA, and the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply.

6. How long we keep it

Usage statistics are kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months. Resend keeps records of emails it delivered for a limited time under its policy. We keep your other data while you have an account, and our database is backed up automatically: daily backups are kept for 7 days and weekly ones for 8 weeks, so anything deleted is gone from backups within 8 weeks. If you delete an entry or a practice, it's removed from our database straight away. If you delete your account, all your practices, entries, settings and your sign-in account are deleted. Our providers may hold copies in their own secured logs or backups for a limited time under their policies.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Access a summary of your data and how it's processed. You can see all of it in the app, and download it with Setup › Data › Export as JSON.
  • Correct or update it: edit or delete practices and entries in the app, or ask us.
  • Erase it: delete entries and practices in the app, or everything with Setup › Danger zone › Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent: for emails, use the unsubscribe link in any email or Setup › Notifications and privacy; for usage statistics, turn them off in Setup › Notifications and privacy (which also removes the Analytics cookies), or choose Opt out on our website; for phone reminders, turn them off in Setup or in your browser's settings; for everything else, delete your account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity, by writing to us.
  • Raise a grievance with us (section 11). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each signed-in person read and write only their own data, and App Check helps make sure only the real TikTym app can reach it. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach affects you, we'll inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

9. Children

TikTym is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly process children's data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if our practices change; the date at the top shows the latest version. For significant changes we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.

11. Contact and grievances

For questions, requests or grievances about your personal data, write to our Grievance Officer at praveen.cherukuru@gmail.com. We'll acknowledge your message and respond as quickly as we can, and always within the time the law requires.
