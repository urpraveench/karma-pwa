How it works

Three steps, every day.

  1. Choose your practices

    Course study, reading, exercise, meditation, or anything you want more time for. Give each a daily target in minutes, and the days it applies.

  2. Log or time your minutes

    Start the focus timer and the minutes log themselves, or tap once to log what you did. Every minute is a point.

  3. Clear your day, and what's carried

    Fill the day's ring of 108 beads. Miss a day and it carries forward, but only up to a limit you choose, so a busy week never becomes a hole.

Features

Built for busy weeks.

Most habit apps punish a missed day with a broken streak. TikTym keeps the time you owe yourself, sensibly.

Your day at a glance

Today shows every practice, what's done and what's left, and a ring of 108 beads, like a mala, that fills in each practice's colour as you go.

  • One tap logs everything left for a practice
  • Streaks for days you clear completely
  • A daily card that adapts: a comeback after time away, a weekend push, your usual time

A focus timer that logs for you

Start a timer on any practice. One bead lights up per minute, the screen stays awake, and a chime tells you when you've hit your target. Stop, and the minutes are logged.

  • Pause, resume, or keep going past the target
  • Keeps running if you close the app
  • A mini-player follows you around the app

A backlog that forgives

Missed your 30 minutes today? They carry forward to tomorrow, shown in red, so the time you meant for yourself isn't lost. But only up to a limit, two days by default, so a sprint, a release or a trip never buries you.

  • Choose the limit: none, 1, 2 or 3 days
  • Log for yesterday or earlier and everything recalculates
  • Archive a practice and its backlog clears

Insights that coach you

See what's slipping and what's improving: your best and weakest days, your streaks, this week against the same point last week, and a 16-week calendar of every day you cleared.

  • Plain-language coaching: "Meditation is slipping"
  • Next milestones, such as your next streak and hours of practice
  • Daily charts for 7, 30 or 90 days

Install

On your home screen in 10 seconds.

TikTym is a web app: no app store, no download, always the latest version. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen, like any other app.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open TikTym in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open TikTym in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it appears, or the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.
  3. Open TikTym from your home screen.

FAQ

Questions

How is TikTym different from other habit apps?

Most habit apps count ticks and streaks, so one busy day breaks your run, and a busy week makes you give up. TikTym is built for people with demanding jobs. Every minute counts, so ten minutes on a hectic day is still progress. Missed minutes carry forward so you can catch up on a calmer day, but only up to a limit, so they never pile up. And learning, health and a calm mind share one daily target instead of a long list of habits. It's free, has no ads, works offline, and keeps your data in India.

Is TikTym free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paid plan. If we ever add paid features, we'll say so clearly before anything is charged.

What happens if I miss a day?

The minutes carry forward to the next day, so they're not lost. To stop a bad week becoming a mountain, a practice carries at most two days of its target by default. You can change it to one or three days, or choose None for no limit.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Once you've opened it, TikTym works offline and syncs your entries when you're back online.

Is there an Android or iPhone app?

TikTym is a web app you add to your home screen (see Install). It opens full screen and works like an app. A Google Play listing is on the way.

Can I use it on my laptop and phone?

Yes. Sign in on both and your practices and entries stay in sync.

Who can see my data?

Other users can't: each account can reach only its own data. It's stored in India, never sold or shared, and you can export or delete it any time. See the Privacy Policy.

Is this medical or fitness advice?

No. TikTym helps you track and keep time for your goals. Talk to a qualified professional before starting a new exercise or health routine.
