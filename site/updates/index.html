What's new in TikTym

Every update people would notice, newest first. Most of them start with feedback, so tell us what you'd like next.

One tap logs today

Version 1.15.0 · 14 October 2026

  • One tap logs today, not your backlog. The coloured button on a practice now logs just today's minutes, so doing your 60 minutes of exercise logs 60, even with minutes carried over from earlier days. Carried-over minutes get their own red button once today's part is done, so you clear them only when you've really done them. Each practice also shows today's target on its own: "0 of 60, 120 carried".
  • Fewer questions after logging. "How did it feel?" now comes up only after a focus timer session, not every time you log, so the message after a quick tap is short and stays out of the way.
  • Evening check and practice reminders on iPhone. If TikTym is on your iPhone's home screen, the 8 pm "Did you log what you did today?" check and your practice reminders now arrive, as the daily reminder already did.

A reminder for each practice

Version 1.14.0 · 13 October 2026

  • A reminder for each practice. Want a nudge to read at 10 am, or to exercise at 6 pm? Open the practice in Setup and pick a time under "Remind me". It comes only if that practice is still to do that day.
  • Tidier practice editing. When you edit a practice, the date box appears only after you tap Change next to its start date.
  • Clearer when something goes wrong. If TikTym can't finish loading, or can't load your practices, it now says so in plain words with a Reload button, instead of a technical message.
  • Smoother updates. If TikTym was left open while we released a new version, it now moves to the new version by itself instead of getting stuck on the sign-in screen.

Reminders on from day one

Version 1.13.0 · 12 October 2026

  • Clearer times. The "your usual time" card on Today now says "around 7 am" instead of "around 07".
  • Reminders on from day one. Your 7 pm reminder is on from the start. The first time you tap in TikTym, your phone asks to allow notifications; allow it and the reminders arrive. Switch them off any time in Setup.
  • An 8 pm check-in. If you allow notifications and haven't logged anything by 8 pm, TikTym asks: "Did you log what you did today?" Tap the mic and just say it. Switch it off in Setup.
  • Start the day with an idea. The first time you open TikTym each day, today's idea fills the screen for a moment before Today. Tap to go straight in.

Links open where you are

Version 1.12.1 · 12 October 2026

  • Links open where you are. On Android and computers, tapping a TikTym link opens it in the TikTym window you already have, not a second one. On iPhone, open TikTym from your home screen icon so you stay signed in; our emails now remind you.

See your streak

Version 1.12.0 · 11 October 2026

  • See your streak. Under your streak on Today, dots show how this week has gone. Tap it for a calendar of clear days, the days a shield saved, and how close your next shield is. It's also in Insights, next to Consistency.
  • A saved streak looks like good news. When a shield keeps your streak, Today says so in gold with a shield, not in warning red.

Streak shields, moods and a daily idea

Version 1.11.0 · 11 October 2026

  • Streak shields. Clear every practice three days in a row and you earn a shield (you can hold three). If you miss a day, a shield keeps your streak going. The minutes you missed still carry over.
  • Say how it felt. After you log, tap 😞 😐 🙂. Once you've rated a few sessions, Insights tells you which practice leaves you feeling good.
  • Just 7 minutes. On days you haven't started yet, one tap starts a 7-minute timer on your easiest practice.
  • Today's idea. A short new idea about practice and habits at the bottom of Today, every day.

Faster voice updates

Version 1.10.2 · 11 October 2026

  • Faster voice updates. TikTym skips a security check it was repeating on every voice update, so your entries come back sooner.

A voice wave that moves

Version 1.10.1 · 11 October 2026

  • See TikTym listening. While you record, the sound wave now flows the whole time and grows as you speak. With Reduce Motion on, it rises and falls in place.

Log by voice

Version 1.10.0 · 11 October 2026

  • Log minutes from the main microphone. Tap once, speak naturally, and watch the sound bars move while TikTym listens. AI turns updates such as “I did an exercise for 5 minutes today” into editable practice-and-minute suggestions, and nothing is saved until you confirm. Retry the same recording, type with your phone keyboard, or use the familiar minute buttons if voice is unavailable. You can use voice up to 50 times a day.

Daily reminders and your week by email

Version 1.9.0 · 10 October 2026

  • A weekly recap by email, every Monday morning. Last week's minutes, the days you showed up, your best day and how each practice went, with one small suggestion for the week ahead. Turn it off any time in Setup › Notifications and privacy.
  • A short note if you've been away for a few days, with one small way back in: 5 minutes of your shortest practice. Just one per break, and no making up for missed days.
  • A daily reminder on your phone, at a time you choose. After your first minutes of the day, TikTym offers to remind you tomorrow; it names what's still left and stays quiet once your day is clear. Change the time or turn it off in Setup. On iPhone, it works once TikTym is on your home screen.
  • The number of practices left today on TikTym's icon, on iPhone (from the home screen) and on computers where TikTym is installed. It clears when your day is clear. Turn it off in Setup.

100 minutes a day for your productive life

Version 1.8.0 · 10 October 2026

  • Help adding TikTym to your home screen. After your first minutes, a card shows how on your phone: one Install button on Android, the two Safari steps on iPhone, or how to open TikTym in your browser if a link opened inside WhatsApp or Instagram. It's also in Setup any time.
  • A new promise: 100 minutes a day for your productive life. 30 minutes to move, 30 to read, 30 to grow and 10 to breathe, the four practices every new account starts with. Busy days still carry forward.
  • Notifications and privacy in Setup: switches for each kind of email (tips and reminders, weekly recap, news) and for usage statistics. They're on from the start, and each turns off with one tap.
  • A welcome email when you start, with the practices you chose and how to add TikTym to your home screen, and a gentle nudge a day later if you haven't logged your first minutes. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Updated Privacy Policy and Terms explaining what's on and how to turn it off.
